# Diagnostic Framework

Use this when the user wants a useful explanation, not just a number.

## Diagnose in this order

1. Is the data reliable enough?
2. Is the traffic well matched to the offer?
3. Is the offer competitive enough to close the sale?
4. Is the listing strong enough to persuade?
5. Is the product itself attractive enough versus alternatives?

## Main diagnosis categories

### Traffic problem
Wrong audience, weak intent, or poor traffic mix.

### Listing conversion problem
Good audience, weak persuasion.

### Offer problem
Good interest, weak price or buying conditions.

### Product problem
Weak inherent desirability or poor fit with market expectations.

### Misleading-data problem
The number should not be trusted heavily because the sample or context is distorted.
