# Risk Classification

Use this file to classify CPC outcomes.

## Good CPC

Use when:
- current CPC is clearly below the prudent or median supportable range
- the bid does not rely on optimistic conversion assumptions
- the product remains economically safe

## Acceptable CPC

Use when:
- CPC is close to the median supportable level
- the data quality is decent
- the position is defensible but should be monitored

## Dangerous CPC

Use when:
- CPC exceeds the median supportable level and begins to rely on optimistic conversion
- the product becomes fragile if conversion drops
- the economics are exposed to small negative changes

## Misleading CPC

Use when the number looks good or bad on the surface but context changes the interpretation.

Common cases:
1. cheap CPC but weak product conversion
2. expensive CPC that is still economically fine due to strong price and conversion
3. apparently safe CPC built on tiny data volume
4. acceptable CPC that only works under unrealistic assumptions

Always explain why the CPC falls into the selected class.
