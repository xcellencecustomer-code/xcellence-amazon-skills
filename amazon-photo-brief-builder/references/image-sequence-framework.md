# Image Sequence Framework

Use this framework to structure Amazon listing images as a visual conversion path.

## Core principle

The image stack should help the buyer move from:
recognition -> interest -> belief -> reassurance -> clarity

## Default 7-image structure

### Image 1 — Click and recognition
The buyer must instantly identify the product and feel the listing is clean and credible.

### Image 2 — Main benefit
Show the strongest reason to care.

### Image 3 — Second benefit
Reinforce desire with the next most important benefit.

### Image 4 — Proof or differentiation
Show why the product deserves belief or preference.

### Image 5 — Objection handling
Address one of the strongest doubts in the category.

### Image 6 — Projection or reassurance
Help the buyer imagine real use and feel more confident.

### Image 7 — Clarification
Remove confusion about size, variants, compatibility, pack contents, or range.

## Adaptation rule

You can compress or reorder this structure if the category demands it, but preserve the logic:
- attract
- explain
- prove
- reassure
- clarify
