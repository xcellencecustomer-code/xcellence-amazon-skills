# Potential Verdicts

Use these verdict states by default.

## Strong potential

Use when:
- the need is clear
- the value proposition is strong
- the differences matter
- price and value are coherent
- the product has a defendable path to win

## Real potential but needs reinforcement

Use when:
- the base idea is good
- the market need is real
- but proof, positioning, pricing, differentiation, or presentation need strengthening

## Weak potential

Use when:
- the product is too close to existing offers
- the value proposition is weak
- the differences do not justify preference
- or structural weaknesses are likely to hurt conversion

## Misleading potential

Use when:
- the product sounds good on paper
- but the market need is unclear, the value is overstated, the proof is weak, or the competition is too established relative to the offer

## Required verdict explanation

Always explain:
- why the verdict was chosen
- what keeps the product from the next stronger verdict
- what must be checked or improved next
