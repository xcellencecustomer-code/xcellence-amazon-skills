# Evaluation Framework

Use this framework to evaluate a product against the competition.

## Core sequence

Always reason in this order:

1. What problem does the product solve?
2. How important is that problem to the target buyer?
3. What is the product's value proposition?
4. What does the market already offer?
5. Which competitors matter most?
6. Where is the product genuinely stronger?
7. Where is it weaker or fragile?
8. Which differences matter commercially?
9. Does the product have a defensible place in the market?

## Comparison categories

Use these comparison layers:
- need addressed
- main promise
- useful attributes
- differentiation
- perceived quality
- pricing logic
- social proof or review reality
- structural risks

## Competitor grouping

When possible, group competitors into:
- niche leaders
- low-cost alternatives
- premium alternatives
- generic copies
- specialized-use competitors

This prevents shallow "all competitors are the same" conclusions.
