# Keyword Priority

Use this file to decide what deserves title placement.

## Tier 1 — essential terms

These define the product itself.
Examples:
- protège-matelas imperméable
- gourde isotherme
- oreiller mémoire de forme

These usually belong in the title, early.

## Tier 2 — decision-shaping terms

These refine the product and help buying decisions.
Examples:
- bambou
- 160 x 200 centimètres
- anti fuite
- silencieux
- compatible iPhone 15

Keep these when they:
- meaningfully refine the product
- affect the click or purchase decision
- match a likely important query

## Tier 3 — removable or secondary terms

These often belong in bullets, attributes, or backend fields instead of the title.
Examples:
- premium
- haute qualité
- pratique
- confortable
- durable

Also remove:
- repeated synonyms
- redundant phrases
- weak marketing filler
- terms that make the title artificial

## Keep-or-remove filter

Keep a term only if it does at least one of these:
- identifies the product
- captures an important keyword target
- filters the right buyer
- resolves a major objection
- helps the user compare products quickly

If removing the term makes the title no less clear, no less targeted, and no less convincing, remove it.
