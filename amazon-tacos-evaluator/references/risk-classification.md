# Risk Classification

Use this file to classify TACOS outcomes.

## Profitable TACOS

Use when:
- TACOS is clearly below the supportable ceiling
- contribution margin after ads remains comfortably positive
- the result is coherent with business phase
- there is no sign of unhealthy ad dependence

## Acceptable TACOS

Use when:
- TACOS is somewhat tense but defensible
- the context justifies it, such as launch, offensive scaling, or position defense
- the margin is still supportable
- the situation is monitored and intentional

## Dangerous TACOS

Use when:
- TACOS is too close to or above the supportable ceiling
- contribution margin after ads becomes too weak
- the result is structurally fragile
- there is little organic support or poor justification for the spend

## Misleading TACOS

Use when the percentage looks good or bad on the surface but the interpretation is wrong.

Common cases:
1. low TACOS but very weak volume
2. low TACOS carried mostly by organics rather than ad quality
3. apparently acceptable TACOS with weak real margin
4. high TACOS that is strategically acceptable in launch or conquest mode

Always explain why the TACOS falls into the selected class.
