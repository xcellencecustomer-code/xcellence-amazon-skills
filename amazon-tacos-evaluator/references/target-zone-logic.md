# Target Zone Logic

Use this guide to recommend a TACOS zone.

## Step 1: find the ceiling

Calculate the theoretical maximum TACOS from the product margin.

## Step 2: add a safety margin

Do not recommend the ceiling as a target.
Keep room for:
- CPC increases
- conversion drops
- promotions
- returns
- cost imprecision

## Step 3: adapt to the business goal

### Profit mode
Recommend a conservative zone comfortably below the ceiling.

### Balance mode
Recommend a moderate zone that protects both margin and growth.

### Conquest mode
A higher TACOS can be tolerated if it is:
- temporary
- intentional
- monitored
- tied to ranking or share gains

## Step 4: validate with contribution margin after ads

The zone must still leave a satisfactory contribution margin after ads.
If the user has a minimum contribution margin standard, apply it.
Otherwise, present the zone as a decision range and explain the tradeoffs.
