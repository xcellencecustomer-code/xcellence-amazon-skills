# Useful vs Decorative Scenes

Use this guide to decide whether a scene deserves time in the video.

## Useful scene

A useful scene does one or more of these:
- explains the product faster
- shows the product solving a problem
- proves a benefit
- reduces hesitation
- makes use obvious
- creates preference versus alternatives

## Decorative scene

A decorative scene usually:
- looks attractive but teaches nothing
- delays the product reveal
- repeats a message already understood
- shows lifestyle without clear product purpose
- could be removed with little loss of selling power

## Fast test

Ask:
1. What exact sales message does this scene carry?
2. Does the viewer learn something that helps the purchase?
3. Does it prove, reassure, or clarify?
4. Would the video be weaker without it?

If the answer to question 4 is no, the scene is likely decorative.
