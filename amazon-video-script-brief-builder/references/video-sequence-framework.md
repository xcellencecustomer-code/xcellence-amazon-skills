# Video Sequence Framework

Use this framework to structure Amazon video as a short conversion path.

## Core principle

The viewer should move through this logic quickly:
attention -> understanding -> belief -> reassurance

## Default sequence

### Scene 1 — Hook
Show value immediately. Do not waste the opening on slow branding.

### Scene 2 — Main benefit
Make the strongest reason to care obvious.

### Scene 3 — Demonstration
Show the product in use so the buyer understands it without effort.

### Scene 4 — Differentiation or second benefit
Show why this product deserves preference.

### Scene 5 — Objection handling
Reduce one of the strongest doubts or frictions.

### Scene 6 — Final reassurance
Leave the buyer with clarity, confidence, and a strong product memory.

## Compression rule

If the video must be very short, combine scenes, but preserve:
- product value early
- visual proof
- reduced doubt
