# Marketing Angles

Use this after the persona is validated.

## Goal

Turn the persona into concrete messaging directions that can support:
- product listings
- bullets
- ad hooks
- hero messages
- visual concepts
- landing page sections

## Angle template

For each angle, include:

### Angle name
[a short, strategic label]

### Central idea
[what this message is fundamentally about]

### Pain or desire targeted
[what emotional or practical lever it uses]

### Objection reduced
[which hesitation it helps lower]

### Recommended message style
[direct, reassuring, sensory, rational, emotional, practical, etc.]

### Why this angle matters
[why it is commercially useful]

## Ranking rule

Rank angles using:
- immediacy of relevance
- connection to the strongest pain
- objection-lifting power
- clarity
- differentiation value

## Strategic output

After listing the angles, always identify:
- the best first angle
- the best supporting angle
- why the pairing works
